A Pune consultancy and legal practice, since the year MahaRERA began.

Founded on 1 May 2017 by Adv. Amruta Bhosale Salunke, Glitedge guides developers, home-buyers, agents and institutions through registration, compliance, planning and dispute resolution across Maharashtra and Goa.

Before Glitedge, Adv. Amruta spent nearly a decade with Poonawala, Sobha Developers and Brahma Corp — then left a secure corporate career to build a practice dedicated to plain-spoken, accountable guidance.
