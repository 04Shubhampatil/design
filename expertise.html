Quick check — does your project need registration?

Registration is generally required for projects over 500 sq m of land or with more than 8 apartments, and for ongoing projects without a completion certificate. Exemptions are narrow — confirm with an advocate before you advertise or take bookings.

Indicative only, not legal advice.
