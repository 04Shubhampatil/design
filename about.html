About — Glitedge Consultancy Services

A practice built the year MahaRERA began.

Glitedge is a Pune-based consultancy and legal practice founded by Adv. Amruta Bhosale Salunke on 1 May 2017. What began as a specialist RERA desk now guides developers, home-buyers, agents and institutions through registration, compliance, planning and dispute resolution across Maharashtra and Goa.

The founder

Nearly a decade in corporate real estate, then a practice of her own.

Before Glitedge, Adv. Amruta spent nearly a decade with Poonawala, Sobha Developers and Brahma Corp, working inside the development process she now advises on. She left a secure corporate career to build a practice dedicated to RERA — plain-spoken, accountable and led by an advocate on every file.

Today she shares that knowledge beyond the office: through Simplify RERA, her YouTube channel of short explainers in Marathi and English, through webinars for developers and agents, and through law-college lectures. Her work has been recognised by Sakal, Lokmat, Times and Mid-day.
